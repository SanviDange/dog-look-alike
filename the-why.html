The why

The idea for the website stems from a popular theory that I have been noticing particulalry on the streets of New York.
Owners and dogs seem to look a little too alike?


Turns out it's not just a theory but an actual phenomenon according to National Geographic, BBC, American Kennel Club, USA Today, Business Insider, New York Post, CNN, The Guardian, Discover Magazine, The Economist, Monaco Tribune, Reader's Digest ...you get the idea.
