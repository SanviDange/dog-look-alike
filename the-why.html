The how. The match. Dogglegänger

The why

Walk around New York long enough and you start to notice it. Owners and their dogs look a little too alike. It's almost impossible not to see once you start looking (trust me).



Turns out it's not just a theory but an actual phenomenon according to National Geographic, BBC, American Kennel Club, USA Today, Business Insider, New York Post, CNN, The Guardian, Discover Magazine, The Economist, Monaco Tribune, Reader's Digest ...you get the idea.



Most of the studies focus on personality - shared energy, lifestyle, even stress levels bleeding between owner and dog over time. But what if it's pushed further? What if the resemblance isn't just behavioral, but actually visible? Dogglegänger uses facial recognition and machine learning algorithms that analyze facial features and patterns. By comparing a user's photo with a vast database of dog images, the system identifies potential matches and suggests the most likely canine match based purely on physical features and expressions.
